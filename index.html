01 / THE RESEARCHBEYOND THE FIXED ANTENNA

Make the antenna
part of the algorithm.

Conventional hybrid MIMO combines digital and analog processing. Tri-hybrid design adds a third degree of control: the electromagnetic behavior of the antenna itself.

STREAMS → RF CHAINS

LAYER 01 / DIGITAL BASEBAND

Start with the signal.

Digital processing maps information streams to the available RF chains. It adapts precoding or combining to the effective channel created by all three layers.

Design question: how should streams share the available RF chains?

↗

Joint control

Co-design the layers. A change in antenna state can change the channel that digital and analog processing must use.

Physical constraints

Keep coupling, loss, bandwidth, realizable states, and architecture-dependent power in the model.

◎

Evidence first

Evaluate tradeoffs against matched baselines. Simulation results are a starting point for validation, not universal hardware gains.
